04 / Principles

Cause stays connected to effect.

Feedback begins at the press, follows the action, and leaves a clear recovery path.

  1. 01 Cause

    Input receives visible feedback within 100ms.

  2. 02 Direction

    Movement explains where content came from and where it went.

  3. 03 Recovery

    Errors retain work and pair the problem with the next action.

Do

  • Give each task region one obvious primary action.
  • Use semantic tokens instead of isolated raw values.
  • Preserve useful content during refresh and retry.
  • Verify narrow, dark, keyboard, and reduced-motion states.

Do not

  • Use blue as decoration or multiply primary actions.
  • Wrap every paragraph or metric in a floating card.
  • Hide essential actions behind hover alone.
  • Remove focus outlines without a stronger replacement.
