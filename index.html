Operating principles

How comfort becomes confidence.

Three decisions keep the visual language coherent across screens, teams, and implementation details.

  1. One clear next step
    01

    Comfort by default

    Neutral surfaces carry the interface. Blue appears only when action, focus, selection, or navigation needs a signal.

  2. Input Result Cause stays connected to effect
    02

    Direct in action

    Feedback begins where an action happened. Movement explains direction, and every error sits beside a clear recovery path.

  3. AA Readable, reachable, recoverable
    03

    Trust through continuity

    Focus remains visible, useful work survives failure, and the same semantic contract holds in dark, narrow, and reduced-motion contexts.
